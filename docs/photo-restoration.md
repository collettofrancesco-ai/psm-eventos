## Prompt finale

Edit target: the supplied aerial photograph of an actual crowded night concert. Quality restoration only, preserve the exact original composition and framing, same stage and truss layout, existing crowd density and positions, umbrellas, buildings, lighting colors and every existing logo and text (LOLA GROUP, PACEÑA, CELEBREMOS JUNTOS). Increase resolution to approximately 3200 pixels wide, reduce JPEG compression artifacts and grain, carefully sharpen real structural edges and natural textures. Preserve photographic realism and the documentary scene; do not redesign or add objects, people, faces, lights, signage or details that are not evidenced by the source. Preserve unobtrusive distant faces, do not invent facial identities. No new text or UI overlay. Deliver the photo itself at its original landscape aspect ratio, with no border. Prioritize fidelity over aggressive artificial detail.
